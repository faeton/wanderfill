Which of these were visits?

Each stretch below is days on which you have photos inside one region. Answer from what you remember yourself.

  • Leaving a question blank is fine. It holds the trip that it is in, and that trip is not made.
  • A photo does not prove a visit. A stretch begins and ends on a day with a photo, and the days with no photo in it are named.
  • The first or the last day of a stretch can be a flight day: photos in the region were taken at speed, as at an airport. It is in the stretch unless you leave it out. After a night flight a phone can still say the old place, so it can be a day too many.
  • A day beside a stretch can have photos in the region beside an impossible jump, and none at speed. Those say the old place as often as not, so it is out unless you include it.
  • Times are by the photo's own clock.
  • Your answers change nothing by themselves. This page sends nothing anywhere: you save the answers as a file, and a plan is made from it for you to read first.

1 to 6: the stretch goes into its trip, as a visit of that quality.
Leave it out: it goes into no trip. It never removes anything from your profile.
I don't remember: a final answer. The whole trip that the stretch is in is not made.

Some stretches are grey. They are here so that the order is true, and cannot go into a trip.
