What to do

  1. Look at each stretch below. A stretch is some days when your photos are in one region.
  2. If you remember it, press the answer that is true. Answer only from your own memory.
  3. If you are not sure, leave it blank. That is fine.
  4. When you stop, press Download answers at the bottom. Then tell the person who runs the tool that the file trips-answers.json is saved.

This page sends nothing anywhere. Nothing changes on your profile from this page. A plan is made from your file later, and you read it first.

What the answers mean

    Good to know
    • A photo does not prove a visit. A stretch begins and ends on a day with a photo, and the days with no photo in it are named.
    • The first or the last day of a stretch can be a flight day: photos in the region were taken at speed, as at an airport. It is in the stretch unless you leave it out. After a night flight a phone can still say the old place, so it can be a day too many.
    • A day beside a stretch can have photos in the region beside an impossible jump, and none at speed. Those say the old place as often as not, so it is out unless you include it.
    • Times are by the photo's own clock.
    • Stretches that are on your profile already, or cannot go into a trip, are folded away in their place. Open a fold to see them. They are there so that the order is true.
